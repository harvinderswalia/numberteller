Clients rarely end a consultation with "thank you, that is interesting". They ask, "So what can I do?" Remedies are how many practitioners answer that question. This guide covers the main families of numerology remedies, how to choose them, and the ethical lines that protect both your client and your practice.

## What a remedy is, and what it is not

In numerology, a remedy is a supportive suggestion intended to balance an energy that looks weak, missing or excessive in a chart. It might be a colour, an object, a spelling, a number choice or a timing decision.

A remedy is not a cure, a guarantee or a protection against misfortune. It works within numerology's own frame, as a way of directing attention and intention. Present it that way from the first conversation.

Most practitioners group remedies into four families:

1. **Element remedies** based on the Lo Shu grid.
2. **Name remedies** through careful spelling adjustment.
3. **Number remedies** for house, car and mobile numbers.
4. **Timing remedies** based on Personal Year and Personal Month cycles.

## Element remedies from the Lo Shu grid

The Lo Shu tradition links each number to one of five elements. Missing numbers suggest an under-represented element; heavily repeated numbers can suggest an excess.

### The number–element map

| Lo Shu number | Element |
|---|---|
| 1 | Water |
| 2, 5, 8 | Earth |
| 3, 4 | Wood |
| 6, 7 | Metal |
| 9 | Fire |

Some schools attach additional meanings or directions to each number. The map above is the one this library uses. Choose a map and apply it to every client.

### The two cycles

Element remedies rest on two relationships between the elements.

**The productive cycle** (one element feeds the next):
Metal produces Water → Water produces Wood → Wood produces Fire → Fire produces Earth → Earth produces Metal.

**The exhaustive or draining cycle** (one element drains the one that feeds it):
Wood exhausts Water → Fire exhausts Wood → Earth exhausts Fire → Metal exhausts Earth → Water exhausts Metal.

To strengthen a weak element, you can add the element itself or the element that produces it. To stop an element being drained, you reduce the element that exhausts it. To soften an excess, some practitioners add the element that drains it.

### Typical element suggestions

These are traditional examples, not prescriptions:

- **To support Water:** metal objects such as a bowl, wind chime or coins in the North; white, gold or silver accents.
- **To support Wood:** a small water feature in the East or South-East; dark blue or black accents.
- **To support Fire:** live plants or wooden objects in the South; green and brown tones.

Notice the logic. Each suggestion uses the producing element: Metal for Water, Water for Wood, Wood for Fire.

Gemstones appear in many traditions as an optional suggestion. Treat them as a personal choice, never as necessary or curative, and never as an expensive upsell.

## Worked example: Priya Sharma's elements

Our fictional sample client, Priya Sharma, was born on 15 August 1990. Her non-zero digits are 1, 5, 8, 1, 9, 9.

| Number | Count | Element |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | Water |
| 5 | 1 | Earth |
| 8 | 1 | Earth |
| 9 | 2 | Fire |
| 2, 3, 4, 6, 7 | 0 | — |

**Element totals:** Water 2, Earth 2, Fire 2, Wood 0, Metal 0.

**Lines:** the will column 9-5-1 is complete. The action column 2-7-6 is fully empty.

**Reading the balance:**

1. Wood (3, 4) is absent. Water produces Wood, and Water is present, so the chart already has a source. A traditional suggestion is a small water feature in the East or South-East, or dark blue accents.
2. Metal (6, 7) is absent. Earth produces Metal, and Earth is present. A practitioner might suggest a few metal or white items in the workspace.
3. Fire (9) appears twice. Repeats can be read as over-energy. Earth drains Fire in the exhaustive cycle, and Earth is already in the chart, so no strong intervention is needed.

A responsible recommendation here would be one or two inexpensive changes, such as a small bowl of water on an East-facing desk and a metal pen holder. Both cost little and can be removed tomorrow.

The empty 2-7-6 column links to two Metal numbers and one Earth number. That gives the advice a second, consistent reason, which is useful when you explain it.

## Name remedies

A name remedy adjusts the spelling of a name so that its numbers harmonise better with the Birthday and Life Path. Common Pythagorean techniques include doubling a letter, swapping C and K, or adjusting a vowel.

Before suggesting any change, follow the full method in our guide to [name correction with the Pythagorean method](/learn/name-correction-pythagorean-method):

- Calculate Expression and Soul Urge part by part, as this library's convention requires.
- Check harmony between Life Path, Expression, Soul Urge and Birthday.
- Avoid creating [over-energy](/learn/over-energy-in-numerology), where the same root fills several core positions.
- Preserve master numbers where the client already has them.

Be realistic about cost. A legal name change involves documents, banks and official records. Many clients prefer a variation for signatures, social media or business use. Always ask how and where the client will actually use the new spelling.

## Number remedies

House, car and mobile numbers are easier to change than names, and some clients find them a practical remedy. Our guide to [mobile and car number numerology](/learn/mobile-and-car-number-numerology) explains the reductions.

Keep the advice proportionate:

- Do not suggest moving house because of a house number.
- Where a number cannot change, many practitioners suggest a supportive element or colour near the door instead.
- When the client is choosing a new number anyway, offer a short list of harmonious options rather than a single "correct" one.

## Timing remedies

Timing is often the gentlest remedy of all. It changes nothing but the calendar.

Use the [Personal Year](/learn/personal-year-numbers) and Personal Month to suggest when to start, consolidate or finish. For example, Priya's Personal Year in 2027 is 25/7, a reflective year. A practitioner might suggest she researches a business idea during 2027 and launches in a more outward-facing year.

Timing advice should never make a client miss a genuine opportunity or delay urgent action. Frame it as a preference, not a prohibition.

## The ethics of remedies

This is the most important section of the guide. Remedies carry trust, and trust can be misused.

### Never promise cures or guarantees

Do not claim that a remedy will cure illness, secure a marriage, guarantee wealth or prevent misfortune. Say what the tradition associates with the suggestion and leave outcomes open.

### Never sell through fear

Avoid language such as "your number is dangerous" or "this will bring disaster unless…". Fear-based selling damages clients and the reputation of the profession. A difficult number is a theme to work with, not a threat.

### Low-cost and reversible first

Start with suggestions that cost little and can be undone: a colour, an object, a timing choice, a signature spelling. Reserve legal name changes and larger purchases for clients who have thought carefully and want them.

### Refer to the right professional

Health questions belong with a qualified doctor. Legal questions belong with a lawyer, and financial decisions with a qualified adviser. You can still offer numerological context, but say clearly that it is not a substitute.

### Document your advice

Write down what you suggested, why, and the convention you used. Include it in the client's report. Good records keep your advice consistent over years and make it easy to review if circumstances change.

### Know when to suggest nothing

Sometimes a chart is well balanced, or the client is anxious rather than curious. Saying "no remedy is needed" is a legitimate and often kind answer.

## Key points

- Remedies are supportive suggestions, never cures or guarantees.
- Lo Shu elements: 1 Water; 2, 5, 8 Earth; 3, 4 Wood; 6, 7 Metal; 9 Fire.
- Use the productive cycle to strengthen and the exhaustive cycle to understand draining.
- Priya Sharma's grid has Water, Earth and Fire, with Wood and Metal absent.
- Prefer low-cost, reversible suggestions and document every recommendation.
- Refer health, legal and financial questions to qualified professionals.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I have to offer a remedy in every reading?

No. Offer one when the client asks for practical steps and the chart supports a suggestion. "No remedy needed" is a valid answer.

### Should I recommend gemstones?

Only as an optional traditional suggestion, if at all. Never present a gemstone as necessary, protective or curative.

### What if a client wants a remedy for a health problem?

Ask them to consult a qualified doctor first. You may discuss numerological themes, but make clear that numerology does not diagnose or treat illness.
