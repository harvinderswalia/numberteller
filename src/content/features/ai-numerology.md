## The practitioner problem: too many combinations, too little time

One client's name can produce dozens of plausible spellings. Each must be totalled per name part, reduced, checked for master numbers and karmic debt, then compared with the birth numbers.

Doing that by hand for twenty variants is slow and error-prone. Business clients add brand ideas, an owner's chart, sometimes partners. Tarot readers must link each card's number to the chart in consistent language.

The hard part of practice is choosing what to recommend and explaining it well. These tools clear the mechanical work so you can focus there.

## What "AI-assisted" means here, in plain terms

The word "AI" can suggest a lot of things. Here is exactly what it means in NumberTeller.

- **It is a rules-based numerology engine built into the app.** It applies fixed rules: the Pythagorean letter table, fixed reduction conventions, master number and karmic debt handling, and a published set of friendly number groups.
- **It searches.** For a name, it generates small spelling changes and calculates each one. For a business, it combines your keywords into candidate names.
- **It scores.** Each candidate is compared with the birth numbers using harmony labels (Perfect, Friendly or Neutral), with adjustments for over-energy.
- **It composes.** It assembles readable text from interpretation rules: card-by-card tarot breakdowns, numerology bridge sentences and narrative summaries.

It does not know your client or why they came to you. It does not invent numerology rules. It cannot tell you whether a client will use a new spelling, or whether a change is culturally appropriate. Those calls stay with you.

## How the engine searches, scores and composes

The logic is transparent so you can audit it.

**Calculation.** Each name part is reduced separately to a single digit, keeping 11, 22 and 33 at name-part level and noting 13, 14, 16 and 19 compounds. The reduced parts are added and reduced again. Life Path reduces day, month and year separately, then adds them.

**Harmony.** Two numbers are compared by their root, so 11/2 counts as 2. The same root is "Perfect". A pair whose numbers sit in each other's friendly group is "Friendly". Anything else is "Neutral". The friendly groups NumberTeller uses are:

| Number | Friendly group |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 |
| 2 | 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 |
| 3 | 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 9 |
| 4 | 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 |
| 5 | 1, 3, 5, 7 |
| 6 | 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9 |
| 7 | 1, 4, 5, 7 |
| 8 | 2, 4, 6, 8 |
| 9 | 2, 3, 6, 9 |

Different schools publish different friendship tables; read the labels with yours in mind.

**Over-energy.** When the same root fills two or more of BD, LP, EX and SU, over-energy is flagged. In name correction scoring, any pair affected by over-energy is down-weighted, so a "Perfect" match built on a repeated number does not automatically rise to the top.

**Composition.** Text is assembled from rules attached to each number and card. It is a draft for you to edit, not a verdict.

## Worked example: one client across the tools

Take a fictional client, **Rahul Saxena, born 6 August 1987**. All figures below were checked with a short script.

| Position | Working | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Birthday (BD) | Day 6 | 6 |
| Life Path (LP) | 6 + 8 + (1+9+8+7 = 25 → 7) = 21 → 3 | 21/3 |
| Expression (EX) | RAHUL 24 → 6; SAXENA 19 → 10 → 1; 6 + 1 | 7 |
| Soul Urge (SU) | RAHUL (A, U) 4; SAXENA (A, E, A) 7; 4 + 7 | 11/2 |

SAXENA totals 19 before reduction, so a karmic debt compound is noted at name-part level. The Soul Urge is a master 11, read as root 2 for harmony.

Harmony on the five checked pairs: LP 3–EX 7 Neutral, LP 3–SU 2 Friendly, EX 7–SU 2 Neutral, BD 6–EX 7 Neutral, BD 6–SU 2 Friendly. There is no over-energy: the roots are 6, 3, 7 and 2.

**Search and score.** Name Correction would try small changes. Two examples:

- *Rahul Saxenna* (double n): EX becomes 12/3 and SU stays 11/2. LP–EX is now Perfect and every other pair is Friendly. But LP and EX now share root 3, so over-energy is flagged and that pair is down-weighted.
- *Rahull Saxenah* (double l, final h): EX becomes 18/9 and SU stays 11/2. All five pairs are Friendly, with no over-energy. It needs two changes, which is a practical cost to discuss with the client.

**Brand check.** Suppose Rahul wants a jewellery brand with a target number of 6. Testing keyword combinations by the same rules: *Swarna Jewels* gives SWARNA 22 (kept as a master at name-part level) plus JEWELS 20 → 2, so 24 → 6, with a Soul Urge of 3. Against his LP 3, EX 6 is Friendly and SU 3 is Perfect. *Suvarna Jewels*, by contrast, totals 8 and misses the target.

**Compose.** In a tarot follow-up, bridge sentences would link each card to LP 3 and his Personal Year. You decide what to keep.

## Using AI numerology software in a consultation

1. **Calculate the base chart first** and check it against your own working. Agree the spelling used on official documents.
2. **Ask about the goal.** It changes which Expression and Soul Urge targets the search favours.
3. **Run the search and read the top 8.** Look at harmony labels and over-energy flags, not just the percentage.
4. **Filter for real life.** Drop variants the client cannot pronounce, would not sign, or that clash with family norms.
5. **Explain two or three options,** showing the numbers for each.
6. **Use tarot or brand tools only where they serve the question.** Reader Mode lets you add your own insights.
7. **Deliver a client-ready PDF** after reviewing every line.

## Limits and responsible use

- **The engine follows rules; it does not judge.** A top-ranked spelling is the best fit by the stated rules, not a guarantee.
- **Your school may differ.** Friendship tables vary between schools. Pick one convention and apply it consistently.
- **Check the arithmetic on anything you deliver.**
- **Do not promise results.** This is supportive, traditional guidance. It does not guarantee wealth, marriage, health or success. Health and legal questions go to qualified professionals.
- **Respect the client's identity.** A name carries family, religious and legal weight. Offer options; let the client choose.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is NumberTeller's AI a chatbot?

No. It is a rules-based numerology engine inside the app. It searches spelling and keyword combinations, scores them against fixed harmony rules and composes text from interpretation rules.

### Can I see why a variant ranked highly?

Yes. Each variant shows its Expression and Soul Urge, the harmony label for each pair and any over-energy flag. You can recalculate any of them by hand.

### Does the engine change master numbers?

Master numbers are preserved. 11, 22 and 33 are kept at name-part level and at the final total, and treated by their root (2, 4 or 6) only for harmony comparison.

### Can I use it for business and brand names?

Yes. Name Correction works for personal, business and brand names, and the Business Name Suggester builds candidates from your keywords and a target number.

### Will the tarot engine replace my own reading?

No. Reader Mode is designed so the engine suggests and you add your own insights. You choose the tone and edit the narrative before sharing it.
