## The practitioner problem: one name, dozens of spellings

Clients ask whether their name supports their career, marriage or health, and whether a small change would help. Answering well means testing many spellings.

Each candidate means totalling each name part, reducing it, watching for 11, 22 and 33, noting 13, 14, 16 and 19 compounds, then comparing the result with the birth numbers. A slip on one letter changes the whole answer. After the tenth variant, attention fades.

A spelling that fixes one clash can create another, or stack the same number twice. Without a consistent method, two consultations on one name can disagree.

## What AI Name Correction does

- **Real-time harmony analysis.** As you type a name, the tool calculates BD, LP, EX and SU and labels each pair Perfect, Friendly or Neutral.
- **Goal targets.** Choose Career, Relationships, Wealth, Health or Spirituality. Each goal sets preferred Expression and Soul Urge numbers. For example, Career and Wealth targets an Expression of 1 or 8 and a Soul Urge of 1, 6 or 8.
- **Pythagorean micro-mutations.** The engine tries small changes: doubled letters, c/k swaps and vowel adjustments. These keep the name recognisable.
- **Over-energy detection.** Variants that place the same root number in two or more core positions are flagged and scored down.
- **Harmony-weighted scoring.** Candidates are scored using the friendly groups NumberTeller uses, then ranked.
- **Top 8 ranked variants** with percentage alignment scores, so you can compare options side by side.
- **Master numbers preserved.** 11, 22 and 33 are kept at name-part level and at the final total.
- **Personal, business and brand names.** The same rules apply to a company name or a product brand.

## How it works: the rules behind the ranking

### Calculation conventions

The tool uses the Pythagorean table (1 = A J S; 2 = B K T; 3 = C L U; 4 = D M V; 5 = E N W; 6 = F O X; 7 = G P Y; 8 = H Q Z; 9 = I R).

Each name part is reduced separately. Master numbers are kept at that level and compounds 13, 14, 16 and 19 are noted. The reduced parts are then added and reduced again, keeping 11, 22 and 33. Some schools add all letters at once; that can give a different compound, so choose one method and apply it to every client.

Life Path reduces day, month and year separately before adding. Birthday is the day of birth reduced.

### Harmony labels

Two numbers are compared by root, so 11/2 is treated as 2. The same root is **Perfect**. Numbers in each other's friendly group are **Friendly**. Anything else is **Neutral**.

Different schools publish different friendship tables; note where yours differs.

The pairs examined are LP–EX, LP–SU and EX–SU, plus the Birthday against the name numbers.

### Over-energy

Over-energy is flagged when the same root fills two or more of BD, LP, EX and SU. Traditionally, too much of one vibration lets its traits tip into excess: a doubled 1 can push independence into stubbornness, a doubled 6 can turn care into over-responsibility. When over-energy affects a pair, that pair's harmony is down-weighted. So a "Perfect" match built by repeating a number does not automatically win.

## Worked example: Kunal Bose, born 8 March 1990

Kunal is a fictional client. All figures were verified with a Python script.

The full-digit method (0+8+0+3+1+9+9+0 = 30 → 3) gives the same root here. That will not always be true.

Both name parts total 14, so karmic debt 14 is noted at name-part level. It is not a final compound here.

### Step 3: harmony for the current name

| Pair | Numbers | Label |

Roots are 8, 3, 1 and 6, so there is no over-energy. Note the goal check too: for Career and Wealth, EX 1 and SU 6 already sit inside the targets. The weakness is the two Neutral pairs.

### Step 4: candidate spellings

| Variant | Change | EX | SU | Neutral pairs | Over-energy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kunal Bose (current) | none | 1 | 15/6 | 2 | none |

**Kunal Bosse.** BOSSE totals 15 → 6, so EX = 5 + 6 = 11, a master number kept as 11/2. The Soul Urge is unchanged. All five pairs become Friendly. It meets the harmony test, though EX moves away from the Career and Wealth targets.

**Kunal Bosee.** BOSEE totals 19 → 10 → 1 and its vowels total 16 → 7. Both are karmic debt compounds at name-part level, worth mentioning. EX becomes 6 and SU becomes 11/2. All pairs are Friendly.

**Kunnal Bose.** EX becomes 6, matching SU 6, so EX–SU reads Perfect. But EX and SU now share root 6. Over-energy is flagged and the pair is down-weighted, which is why this variant ranks below the cleaner options.

## Limits and responsible use

- **It ranks by stated rules only.** A top variant is the best fit by those rules. It is not a promise of success, wealth or marriage.
- **Pronunciation matters.** Many practitioners hold that a spelling change only works if the client uses it. The engine cannot judge that.
- **Respect identity and law.** Changing official documents has legal and family consequences. Suggest a signature or business spelling first where appropriate.
- **Schools differ.** Friendly tables and vowel rules vary. Apply one convention consistently.
- **Health concerns go to a doctor.** A name change is not treatment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is this a lucky name spelling calculator?

It is more than a calculator. It tests many numerology name variants against the client's BD and LP, labels harmony and over-energy, and ranks the top 8. Whether a name is "lucky" remains your interpretation.

### How does it treat master numbers?

11, 22 and 33 are kept at name-part level and in the final total. For harmony they are compared by root: 11 as 2, 22 as 4, 33 as 6.

### Why might a Perfect match rank lower?

If a Perfect pair comes from the same root filling two core positions, over-energy is flagged and that pair is down-weighted. Balance is favoured over repetition.

### Can I use it for a company or brand?

Yes. The same Expression and Soul Urge harmony rules apply to business and brand names, and options from Business Numerology can be tested here.

### Does it decide which spelling the client should use?

No. It searches and scores. You weigh the goal, the number of changes, pronunciation and the client's wishes before recommending anything.
